# Bu & Bu: Bun Dash

A side-scrolling platformer starring Bu & Bu. Collect buns and hearts, bonk the butter blocks, hop on grumpy rain clouds, and reach Bu Café where your Bu is waiting with fries.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser. Everything (art, code, sound) is in that one file.

## Controls
- Move: ← → or A / D
- Jump: Space or ↑ (hold to jump higher)
- Pause: P · Sound: M
- Phones and tablets get on-screen buttons. Turn a phone sideways for a full-screen view.

## Characters
- **Lavender Bu** jumps higher.
- **White Bu** runs faster.
